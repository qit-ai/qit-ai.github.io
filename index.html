01 Overview

About

Quantum information theory (QIT) has long provided a precise language for entanglement, channels, and computational hardness. In parallel, advances in machine learning and foundation models have reshaped how we represent and reason about complex systems. This workshop brings these communities together to examine where each field can inform the other.

We aim for a compact, discussion-oriented meeting: invited talks that clarify conceptual links, short contributions on emerging techniques, and time for open problems. Topics range from quantum learning theory and entropic methods to the use of AI in quantum experiment design and the limits of classical simulation.
